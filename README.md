# HORN OK PLEASE (HOP) — Desi Radio & Mashup Platform

**Bura Mat Mano, Music Hai! • Bollywood × Global Radio**

An ultra-vibrant, high-energy Desi music and mashup radio platform inspired by `hornokplease.wtf`. Features retro Indian truck art design language, an interactive radio station frequency tuner, 1-tap "HONK THE HORN" sound effect micro-interaction, cassette wheel tape spin animations, real-time audio spectrum visualizer, and continuous 24/7 station streaming.

🌐 **Live Vercel Production App**: [https://spotify-who.vercel.app](https://spotify-who.vercel.app)  
📦 **GitHub Repository**: [https://github.com/MJforti/aura-music-player](https://github.com/MJforti/aura-music-player)

---

## 🎨 Key Features & Desi Aesthetics

- **1-Tap "HONK THE HORN! 📢" Synthesizer**: Web Audio API dual-tone Indian truck horn sound effect (`340Hz` & `440Hz` sawtooth wave pair).
- **Interactive Radio Station Frequency Tuner (`StationTuner.tsx`)**: Dial across 5 24/7 continuous Desi channels:
  - **`📻 98.4 FM - HORN OK RADIO`** (Bollywood × Global Hits: *Husn × Let Her Go*, *Chaleya × Until I Found You*)
  - **`💃 104.2 FM - DESI DANCEHALL`** (High Energy Party & Festival Beats: *Aankh Marey × Shape of You*)
  - **`🌙 92.1 FM - AUTO CHILL`** (Late Night Lofi & Emotional Mashups: *O Maahi × Someone You Loved*)
  - **`⚡ 108.0 FM - PUNJABI SPEED`** (Bhangra Trap × Hip-Hop Crossovers: *Pasoori × Levitating*)
  - **`🔥 89.5 FM - 90s TRUCK DISCO`** (Golden Era Remixes: *Chaiyya Chaiyya × Smooth Criminal*)
- **Retro Cassette Tape Deck & Spectrum Visualizer (`CassettePlayerCard.tsx`)**: Rotating cassette reels, live marquee track ticker, animated 16-bar audio equalizer spectrum visualizer, and playback controls.
